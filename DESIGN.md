# Schings Digital design system

## Direction
A young, premium personal media brand, built around Samuel’s portrait photography. The visual language takes its colours from warm outdoor light, deep blue clothing and natural greens in the supplied photographs. Bold editorial typography and spacious composition keep the work confident and human. Avoid neon purple/cyan gradients and generic AI-style visual cues.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm paper | `#f2eee6` | Main canvas |
| Light paper | `#fbf8f1` | Secondary sections and image captions |
| Ink | `#182334` | Main type and controls |
| Muted ink | `#566171` | Supporting text |
| Deep blue | `#172a40` | Campaign fields |
| Slate blue | `#294763` | Secondary fields |
| Ochre | `#92531b` | Emphasis and calls to action |
| Light ochre | `#e0b773` | Emphasis on deep blue |
| Olive | `#697347` | Small organic details |

No gradients are used. Colours stay flat and print-like.

## Typography

- Display: Bricolage Grotesque, 600–800, with fluid editorial sizing.
- Body and interface: Manrope, 400–800, at readable sizes.
- Short overlines identify sections; body copy stays conversational and direct.

## Layout and interaction

- Image-led homepage with a founder portrait and selected real work.
- Shared responsive navigation and repeated page footer.
- Current services are separated from future physical marketing.
- Custom quote enquiry uses a labelled form and opens a prefilled email.
- Visible focus, skip link, accessible form labels, descriptive image alternatives and reduced-motion support are included.
- Hover movement is restrained and non-essential to understanding.
