# Schings Digital website

A multi-page, static website for **Schings Digital**, the personal media brand of Samuel Takudzwa Chingosho. The design pairs a warm, portrait-led palette with direct, editorial typography. It avoids neon purple and cyan gradients.

## Pages

- `index.html` — brand introduction, current services and selected work
- `studio.html` — founder story, working approach, locations and future direction
- `services.html` — current offer, PA equipment supply area and quote process
- `work.html` — family portrait samples, client flyer design, video work descriptions and testimonials
- `contact.html` — custom quote enquiry form, email and social links
- `404.html` — branded not-found page for static hosting

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser or serve the project with any static HTTP server. No build step or dependency installation is required.

## Confirmed business information

Schings Digital is currently Samuel’s personal brand, not a registered company. He works solo and collaborates as needed. He is based in Kwekwe, Zimbabwe, and Kempton Park, Gauteng, South Africa; he works across Zimbabwe and Gauteng and can travel farther when travel expenses are covered. PA equipment supply is limited to Kwekwe and Gweru. Physical marketing is a future plan, not a current service.

Current services: portrait and small-event photography, digital flyers, video filming and editing, social marketing (content creation, account management and paid social advertising), and PA system operation. Portrait delivery is edited digital images; prints are available on request. Flyers can be delivered for social sharing and print.

The site uses British English, the chosen tagline “Made to be seen.”, and custom quotes. Payment methods are intentionally not shown publicly. The contact form opens a prefilled email to `sammychings27@gmail.com`; it does not submit to a server. No phone number is listed.

## Domain and social

The sitemap and canonical URLs use the temporary domain `sammychings27.xyz`; update them when `schingsdigital.com` is ready. Social links: Instagram and X `@schingsdigital`, and the provided Facebook page `Sammychings27 Digital`.

## Portfolio media

Images supplied for the portfolio are resized and recompressed as JPEGs in `assets/images/portfolio/`, stripping source metadata. The two original Lingfield MP4 files were not copied into the repo because together they are approximately 635 MB. The work page identifies them as filmed and edited samples; add web-sized exports before adding playable video previews.

All client names and supplied testimonial attributions are included with Samuel’s permission. No performance metrics or growth claims are published. The family portrait samples are not represented as commissioned client work.
