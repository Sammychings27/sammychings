# Schings Digital — product brief

## Brand
Schings Digital is the personal brand and image of Samuel Takudzwa Chingosho. It is not a registered business yet; the longer-term intention is to grow it into a company. “Schings” shortens Sammychings27, a childhood nickname and long-time online identity based on Samuel’s name and favourite number 27.

## Purpose
Present Samuel’s current media services, show real examples, explain where services are available, and make it easy to request a custom quote. Build trust through a personal approach, broad practical skills and client feedback without inventing claims or pretending future services are already available.

## Audience
Broad: individuals, businesses, events and organisations in Zimbabwe and Gauteng, South Africa. Samuel is based in Kwekwe and Kempton Park and is willing to travel farther when expenses are covered.

## Current services
- Portrait photography as the main focus; also small parties and baby showers. Edited digital photos are standard; prints can be provided on request.
- Digital flyers for any promotion, delivered for social sharing and print.
- Video filming and editing: event highlights, social reels and promotional videos.
- Social marketing: content creation, account management and paid advertising across social platforms.
- PA system operation. Equipment supply is currently available only in Kwekwe and Gweru.

Physical marketing is a future expansion, not a current offer. Quotes, timelines, payment plans and revision rounds vary by project. Any required travel cost is paid upfront once a quote is accepted. Payment methods are discussed after enquiry.

## Proof
Client work: Lingfield Advent Schools (`@las_culture`), KS Tech Logistix, and Lion Gold Construction & Hardware. The user supplied sample work and authorised display of client names and materials. Family and personal portraits are labelled without implying commissioned client work. No certificates or performance metrics are claimed. Four user-provided testimonials may be quoted with spelling corrected.

## Founder and workflow
Samuel works solo, with occasional collaborators. He began in media in 2022. Clients describe their needs in detail; after accepting a quote, required travel costs are paid upfront. Payment arrangements and revision rounds are agreed per project. Samuel requests feedback before final delivery and makes edits as agreed.

## Brand feel and content
Young, premium, bold, open and reliable. Use British English. Tagline: “Made to be seen.” Avoid neon purple/cyan gradients and generic AI-style branding. Palette should draw from the warm light and deep blue in the supplied portraits. Use an honest first-person voice and identify the founder by his full name.
